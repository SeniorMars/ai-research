## Results

- **[01 — SEFE for two planar graphs](01-sefe-2-polynomial/sefe-2-two-graphs.pdf):** A deterministic polynomial-time algorithm for simultaneous embedding with fixed edges (SEFE) of two planar graphs, with no restriction on the common graph. It also constructs the compatible embeddings, with a conservative bound of $O((N+1)^{60})$.
- **[02 — Almost-everywhere circuit lower bounds for E^NP from sparse 3-SAT algorithms](02-almost-everywhere-circuit-lower-bounds/almost-everywhere-circuit-lower-bounds.pdf):** If sparse 3-SAT runs in $2^{O(V/\sqrt{\log V})}$ time, then some language in $\mathsf{E}^{\mathsf{NP}}$ needs $\Omega(n\sqrt{\log n})$ gates at *every* large input length, not just infinitely often. It adapts the Chen–Lyu–Williams refuter so the SAT query grows only linearly in the gate budget.
- **[03 — Subexponential Extended Frege refutations of unsatisfiable 3-CNFs](03-subexponential-extended-frege/subexponential-extended-frege.pdf):** Taking the supplied grid-contraction construction as input, every unsatisfiable $n$-variable 3-CNF has a $2^{o(n)}$-size Extended Frege refutation. The analogous statement for ordinary Frege remains open, and appendices rule out two natural strategies for it.
- **[04 — Simulating nondeterministic time t√(log t) in alternating time O(t)](04-alternating-time/alternating-speedups.pdf):** Nondeterministic multitape computations of length $U$ can be simulated in alternating time $O(U/\sqrt{\log U})$, so $\mathsf{NTIME}[t\sqrt{\log t}] \subseteq \mathsf{ATIME}[O(t)]$. The proof counts computations by an implicit integer formula and evaluates it by alternation.
- **[05 — Variable orders and program proofs](05-variable-orders-and-program-proofs/variable-orders-and-program-proofs.pdf):** The contraction recursion yields subexponential formulas using any number of variable orders tending to infinity, and this is tight for an explicit family. It also gives an $\Omega(n\sqrt{\log n})$ nondeterministic lower bound for regular-expression equivalence.
- **[06 — Element distinctness requires superlinear circuits, assuming a tensor-network volume bound](06-element-distinctness-circuit-lower-bound/element-distinctness-circuit-lower-bound.pdf):** Element distinctness, which lies in $\mathsf{L} \cap \mathsf{coNTIME}[n]$, needs $c\,n(\log n)^{\sigma}$ gates in fan-in-two circuits over the full binary basis at every large input length. It follows from Theorem 2.2 of OpenAI's $\omega = 9/4$ paper via the standard circuit-to-tensor-network conversion, and the note discusses that theorem's proof. Collision separates $\mathsf{USIZE}[O(n)] \subsetneq \mathsf{NSIZE}[O(n)]$, and more strongly lies outside $\mathsf{Mod}_p\mathsf{SIZE}[O(n)]$ for every fixed prime $p$.
- **[07 — Simulating time T with a space-s oracle in space O(s(T)+T/log T)](07-oracle-space-simulation/oracle-space-simulation.pdf):** A time-$T$ machine with adaptive, freshly written queries to an oracle decidable in space $s$ can be simulated in space $O(s(T)+T/\log T)$, by storing only the long-query answers and regenerating query bits inside Williams's square-root-space simulation. For example, $\mathsf{DTIME}^{\textsf{CNF-SAT}}[o(n\log n)] \subsetneq \mathsf{DSPACE}[n]$.
- **[08 — Superlinear XOR circuit lower bounds and superlinear rigidity for an explicit matrix over F₂](08-xor-circuit-lower-bound/xor-circuit-lower-bound.pdf):** An explicit 0/1 matrix $H_N$ needs $\Omega(N\sqrt{\log N})$ XOR gates (tight up to $(\log N)^{o(1)}$), and lowering its rank over $\mathbb{F}_2$ to $\gamma N$ requires changing $N2^{\Omega(\sqrt{\log N})}$ entries, to our knowledge the first superlinear XOR circuit lower bound for an explicit matrix and the first explicit rigidity bound over $\mathbb{F}_2$ that is superlinear at linear rank. They follow from OpenAI's Lean-verified Fourier lower bound by a trace descent and a projection from $\mathbb{F}_4$ to $\mathbb{F}_2$.
- **[09 — Verifying a matrix transpose on tapes](09-transpose-verification/transpose-verification.pdf):** Under the partial-transposition estimate and Weak ETH, a language in one-sided randomized linear time and co-nondeterministic linear time requires $\Omega(n\sqrt{\log n})$ nondeterministic time at every large valid dimension. A second transfer gives an alternative between this tape lower bound and almost-everywhere polynomially superlinear circuit hardness in $\mathsf{E}^{\mathsf{NP}}$ under the stated geometric hypotheses.
- **[10 — Superlinear circuit lower bounds for All-Edges Sparse Triangle, 3SUM, shortest paths, and connectivity, assuming a tensor-network volume bound](10-all-edges-sparse-triangle/all-edges-sparse-triangle.pdf):** Deciding, for every edge of an $m$-edge graph, whether it lies in a triangle needs $\Omega(m(\log m)^{1+\sigma})$ gates, even for sorted edge lists of 2-degenerate graphs on vertex set $[m]$. The hard core is parallel table lookup: checking $q$ claimed values $x_i = y_{j_i}$ needs tensor volume $\Omega(q(\log q)^{1+\sigma})$, because a sorting certificate that three lookups can check is unique exactly when a list is distinct, and summing it out yields OpenAI's list-distinctness bound. Embedding lookup gives the same bound for counting and detecting triangles, $m$ shortest-path queries in a sparse DAG, single-source distances in unweighted trees, component labeling and connectivity queries in forests, counting connected components, 0/1-weight MST, $\mathbb{F}_2$-rank of 2-sparse matrices, and #2SAT with equality clauses, all of which are in logspace. A direct reduction gives $\Omega(n(\log n)^{1+\sigma})$ gates for 3SUM.
